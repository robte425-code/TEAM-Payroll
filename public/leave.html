PTO and Sick time management

View employee YTD PTO/Sick accrued and used hours. Balances update from payroll and leave logs. New employees need a Provider ID and name (same as Employee pay rates); pay rates default to 0 until you edit them there.

PTO balance notice

On My balances, employees see a banner when their available PTO exceeds this threshold. The notice reminds them how many hours will carry over at year-end (using the PTO max carryover limit below).

Year-end rollover

At the start of each calendar year, unused PTO and sick balances carry forward up to these limits. Used hours reset to zero; excess above the limit is forfeited. Rollover runs automatically the first time leave data is loaded in a new year.
